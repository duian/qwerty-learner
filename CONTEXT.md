# CONTEXT.md — Ubiquitous Language

## Glossary

### Learner

The single target user of this product — a 10-year-old boy at elementary mid-level English (1-2 years of study, ~200 word vocabulary, can spell simple words).

### Chapter

A unit of learning containing 20 words. One chapter = one natural daily goal.

### Daily Goal

The number of chapters the Learner is expected to complete per day. Default: 1 chapter (20 words). Adjustable by the Parent.

### Parent

The adult (product owner) who configures the learning path, selects dictionaries, and monitors progress. Does not have a separate account — uses the same local application.

### Streak

The count of consecutive days the Learner has completed their Daily Goal. Resets to 0 if a day is missed.

### Error Review

A mandatory step before starting new chapters each day. The Learner re-types words previously answered incorrectly until they get them right.

### Youth Dictionary

The subset of dictionaries tagged for 青少年英语 (youth English) — primarily PEP (人教版) textbook series grades 1-9. All other categories (CET4, 考研, code practice, etc.) are hidden from the Learner's interface.

### Progress Overview

A simple parent-facing summary showing: streak count, today's completion status, and current dictionary/chapter position. Not a detailed analytics report.
